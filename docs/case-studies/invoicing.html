Hours, Invoicing & Margin Engine

  • Multi-million-dollar portfolio
  • Director review within 2 business days of close
  • Owner / operator
  • 2020 – present

The situation

A multi-client BPO bills each client monthly for contractor hours across multiple roles, rates, and purchase orders. The lifecycle: collect hours, reconcile against payroll source-of-truth, produce draft billing details and invoices, clear an external client approval gate, issue finals, get paid. Done manually, this consumes analysts and produces disputes.

Constraints

  • External approval gates. Clients approve drafts before finals exist — the workflow has a hard-stop control point that must be auditable.
  • Multiple sources of truth to reconcile. Timecard systems, Paycom payroll exports, approved OT/VTO adjustments — all of which must agree before an invoice is defensible.
  • PO hygiene. PO and PO-line details change across fiscal years; a stale PO number is a rejected invoice.

What I built

An end-to-end billing engine using the same paste-validate-publish pattern as my reporting systems:

  • Ingest: raw Paycom time-detail exports drop in unedited
  • Reconcile: approved OT and VTO applied against source-of-truth hours; discrepancies surface before anything is billed
  • Roll up: hours by client, role, and rate → invoice-ready billing detail
  • Margin view: a GPM heatmap by account, so billing data doubles as the margin-management instrument leadership actually uses
  • Process control: the draft → client approval → final workflow, which I documented into the standing SOP after it resolved a recurring client billing dispute

I also serve as the invoice validation gate for leadership: vendor and contractor invoices reconciled against Paycom same-day, with director-level approval signaled on the numbers.

Why it matters

Billing is where reporting mistakes become money. The system is built so the person running it cannot silently produce a wrong number: raw data is quarantined, reconciliation happens before rollup, and the client approval gate is an explicit workflow state. That's what made it fast — speed came from removing rework and disputes, not from working harder.

Impact

  • 2 business daysfrom month close to invoices submitted for director review (July 2026 close: submitted 7/2 EOD) — repeatedly recognized by the director as the fastest submission in the portfolio
  • 3 daysfastest finals-to-accounting turnaround
  • Same dayinvoice validation turnaround for director approval
  • 12+ monthsconsecutive fully accurate, on-time billing and reporting under weekly and monthly deadlines

A recurring client billing dispute was eliminated by documenting the draft-to-final control — adopted as the standing SOP between finance ops and the client. Every formula is wrapped in validation; errors show as flags, not as wrong invoices.

Artifacts

  • Approval-to-billing workflow — intake, reconciliation, approval, exception, and handoff states
  • Billing control matrix and UAT checklist — the checks required before a draft becomes a final
